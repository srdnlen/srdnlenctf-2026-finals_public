Getting your pass

Who needs a pass?{{ chev }}
Everyone attending on site at Fiera Internazionale della Sardegna: CTF finalists, conference attendees, speakers and staff. The pass is your credential at the registration desk and inside the venue.
How do I get one?{{ chev }}
Create an account, complete your profile once, then generate your pass. You can regenerate it any time before the event, the latest download is the one to bring.
I'm a speaker or staff: my pass looks different.{{ chev }}
Speaker and staff passes carry extra fields (talk title, staff area), a different accent colour, and are provisioned by the organisers. If your role is wrong, contact the organising team and we'll fix it before you print.

On the pass

What's printed on the pass?{{ chev }}
Your name, your nickname, your role, your country and team (if set), and your photo if you uploaded one. It's an A6 card designed to print cleanly in black and white or colour, with a role-coloured band so staff can read your role at a glance.
Can I change my details later?{{ chev }}
Yes. Update your profile and generate the pass again. Your username and nickname are fixed at sign-up; the rest can change.
Photo requirements{{ chev }}
PNG, JPEG or WEBP, up to 3 MB.

Logistics & privacy

Do I have to print it?{{ chev }}
No, a copy on your phone works at the desk. A printed A6 card is handier to wear for the three days, though.
What happens to my data?{{ chev }}
Pass data stays with the organisers (Sardinia Len APS) and is used only to issue your credential and run the event. See the code of conduct for on-site expectations.
Still stuck? Reach the organisers at srdnlen.it or ask at the registration desk during the event.
