Code of conduct

Sardinia Cyber Camp is organised by Sardinia Len APS and is dedicated to a harassment-free, welcoming experience for everyone (competitors, speakers, attendees, staff and volunteers) regardless of background, identity or experience level.

Applies to all attendees 28-30 October 2026 · Fiera Internazionale della Sardegna
01

Be respectful

Treat everyone with respect. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation and personal attacks are not tolerated in talks, during the CTF, in common areas or in online channels tied to the event.

02

Compete fairly

  • Attack only the infrastructure the competition scopes in. Shared event systems, the venue network and other attendees' devices are off limits.
  • Don't disrupt the event: no flooding of shared services, no interfering with other teams, no sharing of flags outside your team.
  • Follow the instructions of organisers and venue staff at all times.
03

Respect the venue

Fiera Internazionale della Sardegna is a shared exhibition space. Look after it, follow on-site safety guidance, and keep access routes and fire exits clear.

Reporting a problem

If you experience or witness a breach of this code, contact a member of staff at the registration desk or reach the organisers through srdnlen.it. Reports are handled discreetly. Organisers may take any action they consider appropriate, including removal from the event without refund.

By attending, you agree to follow this code of conduct for the duration of the event.
